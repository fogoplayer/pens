## Cinnamon Rolls {#cinnamon-rolls}

### ChatGPT Version {#chatgpt-version}

1C warm water  
¾ tbsp Yeast  
3 Tbsp oil  
1 egg  
1/4C Sugar  
¼ C Powdered milk  
1 tsp salt  
4C Flour, knead more into the loaf as needed

Stir warm water and yeast together gently, let sit for 5 min  
Add wet ingredients, then dry ingredient

Knead dough until homogenous, adding flour until not sticky, let rise for 5-10 min

Roll out on floured surface

Brush melted butter on top, add cinnamon mixture

Roll up, slice, and place in baking pan. Let rise for 45 min, then bake on 350 for 20 min.

#### Cinnamon Mixture {#cinnamon-mixture}

½ cup softened butter  
½ cup brown sugar  
2 tablespoon ground cinnamon  
1/8 teaspoon salt

Mix together until smooth; heating may be required to make spreadable

#### Cream Cheese Frosting {#cream-cheese-frosting}

4oz softened cream cheese  
1/4C softened butter  
3/4C powered sugar  
2-3 tbsp milk  
1 tsp vanilla

Beat cream cheese and butter until smooth. Add everything else.

### Melissa Loosli {#melissa-loosli}

Water and yeast  
Let sit for 5-10 min  
Everything else  
Whisk until powdered milk is no longer clumpy  
Add flour 4C at a time

### Glaze {#glaze}

½ C Butter  
1tsp vanilla  
2 C powdered sugar  
¼ milk  
Add more powdered sugar until desired consistency
