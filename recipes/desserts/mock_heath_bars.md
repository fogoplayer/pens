## Mock Heath Bars {#mock-heath-bars}

2 sticks butter  
1 c. sugar  
Saltine/Gourmet Crackers  
1 (12 oz.) bag chocolate chips

Boil butter and sugar 3 minutes. Spray a cookie sheet with "Pam" and lay crackers over bottom. Pour hot mixture over them. Bake at 350 degrees for 15 minutes (until it is hot and bubbles). Pour the bag of chocolate chips over the hot mixture and as they soften, spread to cover. Cool and break into pieces. Easy to make and delicious.
