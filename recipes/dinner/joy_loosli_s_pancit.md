## Joy Loosli’s Pancit {#joy-loosli’s-pancit}

2 c cooked, shredded chicken breast  
1 lg onion, diced  
1 c chopped celery  
1 green bell pepper, chopped  
2 carrots, peeled and shredded  
1/2 medium cabbage, chopped  
1/4 c olive oil  
2 pkg chicken flavored ramen  
1/81/4 c soy sauce  
2 c water

Saute onion, celery, pepper, carrots until thoroughly cooked; add cabbage and saute 5-7 minutes, stirring often. Add chicken.  
In a separate bowl, dissolve ramen flavoring in water; stir in soy sauce. Pour over vegetable mixture and bring to a boil. Crush ramen noodles into boiling mixture; cover and simmer 5-7 minutes.
