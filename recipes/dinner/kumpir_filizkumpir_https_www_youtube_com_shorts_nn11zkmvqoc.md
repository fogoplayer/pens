## Kumpir ([@filizkumpir](https://www.youtube.com/shorts/nn11zKMvqoc))

Potato  
Butter  
Meat, veggies, and cheese (I usually use plant-based steak tips, peas, and mozzarella)  
Salt & Pepper

Bake the potato

- Punch holes along one side with a fork
- Microwave for 10 minutes
- Broil on low for 5 minutes

Split along one side, keeping the skin intact  
Place a pat of butter inside the potato so it can start to melt  
Prep the fillings—brown/sear meat, thaw veggies  
Using the skin as a bowl, whip the flesh into mashed potatoes, folding in meat, veggies, cheese, salt, and pepper

- traditionally, the filling is mixed into a sauce, poured into a cavity in the potatoes, and then the potatoes are folded back on top. For dry fillings, mixing it all in together makes more sense

Flatten top, add cheese, and broil on low for another five minutes
