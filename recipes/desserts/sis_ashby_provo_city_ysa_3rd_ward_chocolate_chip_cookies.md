## Sis Ashby (Provo City YSA 3rd Ward) Chocolate Chip Cookies

![][image4]

1C softened butter  
1C crisco  
1.5C white sugar  
1.5C brown sugar  
4 eggs  
2tsp vanilla  
4.5C Flour  
2tsp baking soda  
2tsp salt  
Chocolate chips

Cream the butter, crisco, and sugar  
Add eggs and vanilla, and mix  
Mix flour, baking soda, and salt separately, then mix in  
Mix in chocolate chips and bake at 350 until golden edges
