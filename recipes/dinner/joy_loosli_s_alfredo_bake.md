## Joy Loosli’s Alfredo Bake {#joy-loosli’s-alfredo-bake}

1/2 stick melted butter  
1 10 oz? can of chicken soup  
1 c shredded parmesan cheese  
1 c cooked shredded chicken (optional)  
8 oz. Noodles, cooked  
2 c shredded cheddar or cheddar/jack cheese

Preheat oven to 350 °.  
Cook noodles as directed; drain.  
Heat butter, soup in saucepan; stir in parmesan; heat 'til melted. Stir in chicken and noodles. Put in 2 qt. casserole dish; sprinkle shredded cheese on top. Bake 30 min. at 350 °.
