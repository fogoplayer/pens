## Brownies {#brownies}

1 2/3 cups granulated sugar  
2 tsp. vanilla extract  
1/4 tsp. salt  
3/4 cup (1 1/2 sticks) butter or margarine, melted  
1 1/3 cups all-purpose flour  
3/4 cup chopped nuts (optional)  
2 Tbsp. water  
3/4 cup Baking Cocoa  
Powdered sugar  
2 large eggs  
1/2 tsp. baking powder

PREHEAT oven to 350° F. Grease 13 x 9-inch baking pan.

COMBINE sugar, butter and water in large bowl. Stir in eggs and vanilla extract. Combine flour, cocoa, baking power and salt in medium bowl; stir into sugar mixture. Stir in nuts. Spread into prepared baking pan.  
BAKE for 18 to 25 minutes or until wooden pick inserted in center comes out slightly sticky. Cool completely in an on wire rack. Sprinkle with powdered sugar. Cut into bars. Makes 2 dozen brownies
