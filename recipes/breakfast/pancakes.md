## Pancakes {#pancakes}

| 1x                  | 2x                  | 3x                    | 4x                             |
| ------------------- | ------------------- | --------------------- | ------------------------------ |
| 1 c Flour           | 2c Flour            | 3C Flour              | 4c Flour                       |
| 2 Tbsp sugar        | 1/4 C sugar         | 6 Tbsp (\~1/3C) sugar | ½ C sugar                      |
| 1 tsp salt          | 2 tsp salt          | 1 Tbsp salt           | 4 tsp salt                     |
| 2 tsp baking powder | 4 tsp baking powder | 2 Tbsp baking powder  | 8 tsp (\~3 Tbsp) baking powder |
| 1 egg               | 2 egg               | 3 egg                 | 4 egg                          |
| 2 Tbsp oil          | ¼ C oil             | 6 Tbsp (\~1/3C) oil   | ½ C oil                        |
| 1 c milk            | 2 C milk            | 3 C Milk              | 4 C milk                       |

Mix the dry ingredients in a bowl. Beat the egg in a cup, add oil and milk. Combine liquid with dry ingredients and mix just enough that there is no dry flour visible \-- there should be lumps. Cook over medium-high heat in non-stick skillet. After the first pancake, you shouldn't need to grease it.

Note for Missionaries: You probably won’t have enough time to mix, cook, and eat all at once. Make a triple batch on your P Day and refrigerate it; it’ll last you the week.
