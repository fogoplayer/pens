## Crepes {#crepes}

2 eggs  
1 cup milk  
2 Tbsp butter, melted  
1/4 tsp salt  
1 cup flour

Mix in the order listed. You're supposed to let it stand for an hour; I usually just make the batter the night before. Scoop into a hot skillet 1/4 cup at a time, roll your wrist to spread it out, flip when the top starts looking bubbly and dry. You'll get a feel pretty quickly for whether to turn the heat up or down and how long to cook on each side. Like pancakes, you shouldn't need to grease the pan other than for the first one.
