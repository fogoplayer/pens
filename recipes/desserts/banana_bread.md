## Banana Bread {#banana-bread}

½ Cup Butter or Margarine  
¼ teaspoon Salt  
1 Cup Sugar  
2 Eggs  
3-4 Bananas, Mashed  
4 Tablespoon Sour Cream  
2 Cups Flour  
1 teaspoon Soda

Cream butter, salt and sugar. Add eggs, bananas and sour cream. Sift flour and soda 3 times and add.  
Pour batter into a heavily greased breadpan, pushing it towards the edges to compensate for the center rising.

Bake 325\* for 1 hour and 15 minutes. Makes 1 loaf.

### Joy Loosli’s Recipe {#joy-loosli’s-recipe}

| ½ c butter (softened) 2 eggs ¾ c sugar 1 tsp baking soda 2 tbsp milk ½ tsp salt | 1½ c (about 4 bananas) mashed bananas or applesauce 2 c flour (I usually do 1 c wheat & 1 c white) Optional: 1 c chocolate chips in the banana recipe (not optional in our family) ½ c chopped nuts in either recipe |
| :------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | :------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |

Preheat oven to 350 degrees. Mix together the first 6 ingredients. Add the bananas or applesauce. Gradually add the flour and stir well. Mix in the chocolate chips and/or nuts. Pour into a 9 x 13 greased pan and bake for 25 minutes at 350 degrees. You can also fill muffin cups about ⅔ full and bake at the same temp for 15 \- 18 minutes, until they're a light golden color on top. I just think the 9 x 13 pan is easier.
