## Buffalo Chicken Dip {#buffalo-chicken-dip}

INGREDIENTS:

1 8oz package cream cheese, softened  
½ cup buttermilk ranch dressing  
½ cup buffalo style hot sauce  
½ cup shredded mozzarella chease  
2 10-oz cans of Chicken Breast

Heat oven to 350 F. Place cream cheese in a deep baking dish and stir until smooth. Mix in buttermilk ranch dressing, buffalo style hot sauce and cheese. Stir in chicken.

Bake 15 to 20 minutes or until the dip is completely heated throughout. Stir again and serve.
