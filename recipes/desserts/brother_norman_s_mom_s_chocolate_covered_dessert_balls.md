## Brother Norman’s (Mom’s) Chocolate-Covered Dessert Balls {#brother-norman’s-(mom’s)-chocolate-covered-dessert-balls}

Anyway, on to the recipe:

It's something Mom made up, and fairly simple. Her version is to take a box of german chocolate cake mix and bake the cake (this usually involves eggs, water, oil, and/or milk in addition to the cake mix), then once it's cooked to crumble it up, then stir in a bag of coconut and a tub of frosting (either german chocolate or caramel).

Once it's all mixed together, roll into walnut-sized balls, put them into a bowl with a lid, then put into the freezer until they firm up. (Overnight is ample time.)

Next you need cooking chocolate. She prefers almond bark chocolate (which is 'almonds not included' for some reason), chocolate wafers work too, anything that can melt and still be okay more-or-less. You melt them. Can be done in a microwave-safe bowl in the microwave, in a crockpot, in a fondue pot, etc.

Once the chocolate is melted, start dipping the balls in the chocolate and have them dry on waxed paper or aluminum foil, which takes 10-15 minutes. Two spoons will let you dip the balls with relative ease. They can be stored in the fridge or freezer once dried.

Versions I've done so far:  
\-Original Recipe  
\-Walnut Brownie cake with coconut/pecan frosting  
\-Strawberry cake with raspberry filling (needs to be frozen after the chocolate dries if not eaten right away, filling is wetter than frosting).

Variations I've thought about but haven't done yet:  
\-Lemon cake  
\-Adding peanut butter to the melted chocolate
