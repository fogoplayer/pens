## Melissa Loosli’s Poppyseed Bread {#melissa-loosli’s-poppyseed-bread}

3 ½ C Flour  
1 ½ tsp salt  
1 tbsp water  
1 ½ C oil  
1 ½ tsp vanilla  
1 ½ c milk  
1 ½ tsp almond extract  
1 ½ tsp butter flavoring  
2 ½ C sugar  
1 ½ tsp baking powder  
3 eggs  
1 tbsp poppy seeds

Blend all together. Grease 2 bread pans. Pre-head oven to 360\*. Pour into greased pans. Cook for 1 hour. Take out of pans and let cool. Poke holes in top with toothpick.
