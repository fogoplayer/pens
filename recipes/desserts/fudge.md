## Fudge {#fudge}

3 cups semi-sweet chocolate chips  
1 can sweetened condensed milk  
1 dash of salt  
½ to 1 cup of chopped nuts  
1/2 tsp vanilla

In saucepan, over low heat, melt chips with condensed milk and salt.

- Alternatively, microwave, stirring every 20 to 30 seconds

Remove from heat, stir in nuts and vanilla.  
Spread evenly into wax paper lined 8 or 9 inch square pan.  
Chill two hours until firm, turn fudge onto cutting board, peel off paper and cut into squares, store covered in refrigerator.
