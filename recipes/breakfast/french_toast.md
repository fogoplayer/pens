## French Toast {#french-toast}

4-6 eggs  
1 Tbsp sugar per egg  
2 Tbsp milk per egg  
1/4 \-1/2 tsp vanilla (this makes all the difference)  
Cinnamon \-- sprinkle as much as you like into the batter  
Bread

Beat everything together (the cinnamon works better if you beat it into the eggs before the milk, but it doesn't really matter). Dip bread, then fry on both sides like a pancake. Old fashioned white bread makes the best french toast. Not wonder bread (too soggy/mushy), not whole grain (too heavy). The recipe was invented to make stale bread edible, and it really is better if you let the bread age/dry out a little. Or just go to the clearance section at the bakery in the grocery store and find some white bread that's already a few days old. Eat with syrup, jam, honey, whatever.
