## Pita Pocket Bierocks {#pita-pocket-bierocks}

1/2 lb. ground hamburger or turkey  
1 package onion soup mix  
1/2 head green cabbage, chopped  
1 onion, diced  
2 tsp. seasoning salt  
2 tsp. ground pepper  
1 1/2 tsp. garlic powder  
1 package pita pockets, white or Greek

Cook hamburger, cabbage, onion, soup nix, and seasonings together in a large skillet until cabbage and onions have a cellophane (translucent) appearance. Drain off excess grease. Cut pita pockets in half and warm in toaster, then fill with Bierocks mixture, about 2 heaping tablespoons in each pita pocket. Submitted by: Jody Williams
