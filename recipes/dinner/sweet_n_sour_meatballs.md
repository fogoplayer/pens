## Sweet ‘N’ Sour Meatballs {#sweet-‘n’-sour-meatballs}

3-6 lbs meatballs (Impossible is better than Beyond in 2026\)  
Serves 10

### Sauce {#sauce}

2T cornstarch  
1/2C brown sugar  
1 can of pineapple chunks (drained)  
1/3C vinegar  
1T Soy Sauce  
1/2C chopped bell peppers, onion, and/or ginger

Mix cornstarch and brown sugar. Add pineapple **juices**, vinegar, and soy sauce until smooth. Pour in a pan, stirring until it boils and thickens

Add meatballs, pineapple, and peppers and heat them through
