## Melissa's crock pot Chicken and Veggies {#melissa's-crock-pot-chicken-and-veggies}

1lb Chicken breasts  
2-3 potatoes, peeled and cut  
1 onion, chopped  
2-3 carrots, peeled & cut  
1 can cream of chicken soup  
¼-½ cup water

Place chicken breasts in a roasting pan.  
Add Potatoes & carrots.  
Pour Chicken Soup & water  
Cover and bake @ 375 for an hour, OR crock pot on high for 5-6 hours

Mom’s notes: you can double the veggies and it still tastes good. You’ll need salt & pepper

Serves 2-4
