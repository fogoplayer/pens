## Swedish Pancakes {#swedish-pancakes}

You probably want a double batch at this pan size

3 eggs  
1/2 C. flour  
1 c. milk  
12 tsp. salt  
½ c. butter

Heat the oven to 400°. Put butter in 11 x 17-inch pan and melt in oven. Mix together eggs, flour, milk and salt and pour in pan over butter. Bake 2025 minutes. Top with powdered sugar and serve with syrup.
