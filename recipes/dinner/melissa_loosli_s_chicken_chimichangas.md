## Melissa Loosli’s Chicken Chimichangas {#melissa-loosli’s-chicken-chimichangas}

Cook and dice 1

Roll out rhodes bread dough and cut into 2:1 rectangles  
Put 2 tsp salsa, 1/4c cooked, diced chicken on one side

Fold over into squares and pinch edges.

Bake at 350 for 20 min

Onion and garlic optional, Mom usually doesn’t include them
