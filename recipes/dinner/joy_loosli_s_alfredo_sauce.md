## Joy Loosli’s Alfredo Sauce {#joy-loosli’s-alfredo-sauce}

ALFREDO SAUCE RECIPE

Ingredients  
1/2c butter  
1 pint heavy whipping cream\*  
8 oz cream cheese  
1 tsp garlic powder  
1/4 tsp salt  
1/4 tsp pepper  
1 cup grated parmesan cheese  
1 c cooked shredded chicken

Instructions  
In a medium saucepan add butter, cream, and cream cheese. Cook over medium heat and whisk until melted. Add spices and parmesan cheese; whisk until smooth. Stir in chicken; heat for 3-5 minutes. Serve over pasta.
