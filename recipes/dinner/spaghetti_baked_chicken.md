## Spaghetti-Baked Chicken {#spaghetti-baked-chicken}

4 half chicken breasts, chopped  
2 cans cream of chicken/cream of mushroom soup  
1 can mushrooms  
1 ½ C sour cream

Blend soup, mushrooms, sour cream, chicken

1 ½ C shredded cheddar or jack cheese  
1 lb cooked spaghetti

Layer in a large oblong pan— noodles, saucy chicken, and cheese, twice

Bake at 350 for 30-45 min
