## Joy Loosli’s Creole Jambalaya {#joy-loosli’s-creole-jambalaya}

3 tablespoons cooking oil  
10 ounces (300 g) andouille sausage,sliced into rounds  
1 pound (500 g) boneless skinless chicken breasts or thighs,cut into 1 inch pieces  
1 onion, diced  
1 small green bell pepper ,seeded and diced  
1 small red bell pepper,seeded and diced  
2 stalks/ribs celery,chopped  
4 cloves garlic,minced  
14 ounces (400 g) can crushed tomatoes  
1 teaspoon salt  
1/2 teaspoon ground black pepper  
1 teaspoon each dried thyme and dried oregano  
1/2 teaspoon hot pepper sauce  
2 teaspoons Worcestershire sauce  
1 1/2 cups uncooked white rice(short grain or long grain)  
3 cups low sodium chicken broth  
1 pound (500 g) raw shrimp/prawns tails on or off, peeled and deveined  
Sliced green onions and chopped parsley, to garnish

Saute sausage and chopped chicken in 1/2 of the oil; remove from pan and set aside.  
Saute onion, bell pepper, celery and garlic in remaining oil until thoroughly cooked. Add tomatoes, chicken broth and spices. Bring to a boil. Stir in shrimp, chicken, sausage and uncooked rice; lower temperature and simmer 15-20 minutes, or until rice is cooked.
