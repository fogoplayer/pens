## Joy Loosli’s Tater Tot Casserole {#joy-loosli’s-tater-tot-casserole}

INGREDIENTS:  
1 lb. Ground beef (or ½ cup butter?)  
1 diced onion  
1 c diced celery  
1 diced bell pepper  
1 can cream of mushroom soup  
1 c sour cream  
1 bag (24 oz) tater tots  
2 c grated cheddar or cheddar/jack cheese

Brown ground beef in pan with onion, celery and pepper. Stir in soup and sour cream.  
Place tater tots in 9 X 13 baking dish. Spread beef mixture over tots. Sprinkle cheese over the top. Bake 30 minutes uncovered at 350°.
