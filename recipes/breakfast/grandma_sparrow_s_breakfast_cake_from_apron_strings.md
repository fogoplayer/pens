## Grandma Sparrow’s Breakfast Cake (From _Apron Strings_) {#grandma-sparrow’s-breakfast-cake-(from-apron-strings)}

From the Sparrow family

| 3 cups flour (we use 2 white and 1 wheat) 1½ cups sugar 2 tbsp. baking powder 1½ tsp salt ½ cup butter or margarine 1½ cups milk 2 eggs | Topping ½ cup brown sugar ⅓ cup flour 1 tsp cinnamon ¼ cup butter or margarine |
| :-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | :----------------------------------------------------------------------------- |

Preheat oven to 375 degrees. Mix dry ingredients and cut in butter with a pastry blender until crumbly. Mix eggs, milk, and baking powder with remaining mixture and pour into lightly-greased 9 x 13 pan. Mix topping in separate bowl with pastry blender, sprinkle topping over batter, and bake 25-30 minutes.

Kyle Loosli says:  
_“We’ve played around with different ways of making this. Grandma Sparrow (and hence Melissa) makes it by melting the butter into the topping, making a gooey mess that can’t be sprinkled, only dropped in blobs that sink into the batter. The kids love it this way, but once I figured out that it was supposed to be crumb topping, I took it as a challenge to get it to work right.”_
