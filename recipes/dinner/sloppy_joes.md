## Sloppy Joes {#sloppy-joes}

1 pound ground beef  
1/8 cup brown sugar  
1/8 cup mustard  
1/2 cup ketchup

1. Brown the ground beef in a large skillet on medium high heat until no longer pink.
2. Once browned, drain.
3. Add in brown sugar, mustard, and ketchup. Stir to combine.

![][image1]  
Allow the meat mixture to simmer on low for about 5 minutes to combine the flavors.
