## Melissa Loosli’s Ham Croquettes {#melissa-loosli’s-ham-croquettes}

Makes about 15 croquettes

3½ cups (8 dL) ground cooked ham  
2 cups (1/2 L) Thick White Sauce (p. 265\)

- 3T Butter
- 4T flour
- 1C milk
- 1/4tsp salt
- Blend flour into melted butter until smooth. Add milk at once. Cook until (very) thick
- This makes one cup, then add a cup of cream of mushroom

~~1 tablespoon minced parsley (for color, not flavor)~~  
1 tablespoon Dijon mustard (mom doesn't usually include)  
3 tablespoons minced onion  
1/4 teaspoon freshly ground pepper  
1½ cups (3½ dL) freshly made bread crumbs

- Use cheese grater
- Perhaps mix in cracker crumbs or a grain meal in a 50/50 mix

1 egg  
Vegetable oil

Combine the ham, sauce, parsley, mustard, onion, and pepper, in a bowl and blend well. If mixture is slightly warm, chill in the refrigerator until firm. Put the bread crumbs in a shallow dish, and in another dish beat the egg with 1 tablespoon water. Shape the meat into 12-inch balls or cylinders. Roll them in the crumbs, then dip into the egg, and again roll in the crumbs. Let the croquettes dry in the refrigerator for at least 30 minutes to set the coating.

Deep-fry in 360-degree vegetable oil and dry on paper towels
