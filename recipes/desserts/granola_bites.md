## Granola Bites {#granola-bites}

Granola Bites

2 cups oats  
1 cup rice crispies (or other cereal)  
1 cup peanut butter  
1 cup ground flaxseed  
1 cup mini chocolate chips  
⅔ cup honey or agave nectar  
2 tsp pure vanilla extract  
2 tbsp coconut oil

Combine all ingredients together in a large bowl, mixing gently so the crispy rice cereal doesn’t get crushed, until well combined. Drop rounded tbsp sized portions onto a lined cookie sheet. Cool for 1-2 hours. Will keep for \~1 week in fridge and a few months in freezer. Yield: \~30
