## Elder Mackay's Chocolate Chip Cookies {#elder-mackay's-chocolate-chip-cookies}

1 C Sugar  
1 C Brown Sugar  
1 C Butter  
2 Eggs  
1 tsp vanilla  
3 C Flour  
1 Tsp salt  
1 tsp baking soda  
1 tsp baking powder  
Chocolate chips

Cream butter and sugar, add eggs 1 at a time. Add vanilla and mix.  
In a separate bowl mix dry ingredients, then add to wet ingredients in 3 parts.  
Bake a 350 for 7-10 minutes or until slightly brown.
