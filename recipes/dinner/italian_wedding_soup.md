## ITALIAN WEDDING SOUP {#italian-wedding-soup}

1/2 cup Acini di Pepe (Double for less broth, could also be tiny pieces of angel hair)  
1 Tbsp Olive Oil  
1/2 cup Onion, chopped  
1 cup Carrot, chopped  
1/2 cup Celery, chopped  
1 tsp Garlic, chopped  
4 cups Low Sodium Chicken Broth  
6 oz Turkey Sausage, casings removed (or Meatballs)  
3 cups Baby Spinach (halve)  
2 Tbsp Parsley, chopped  
Salt and Black Pepper

1 Heat olive oil in a pot over medium heat. Add onion, carrot and celery and sauté for 4 minutes or until beginning to soften. Add garlic and sauté 1 minute longer.  
2 Add chicken broth and bring to a boil.  
3 Roll sausage meat into  
1/2 inch balls. Add to soup and simmer for 4 minutes or until just cooked through.  
4 Add baby spinach and acini di pepe and simmer for 2 minutes. Add parsley and season with salt and pepper to taste.  
Refrigerate any leftovers.
