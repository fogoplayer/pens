## Uncle Shawn’s Green Bean Casserole {#uncle-shawn’s-green-bean-casserole}

1pkg bacon  
2 cans green beans  
1 can cream of mushroom soup  
Crispy onions

Cook up a package of bacon extra crispy and crush it into bits.  
Then two cans of green beans and one can of cream of mushroom soup and the bacon bits get mixed up in a baking pan.  
Top with crispy onions and bake it at 350 for 20 ish minutes.
