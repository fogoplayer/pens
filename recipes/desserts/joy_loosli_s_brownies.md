## Joy Loosli’s Brownies {#joy-loosli’s-brownies}

Grandma's Brownies  
Ingredients:  
1 cup melted butter  
4 eggs  
1/2 cup cocoa powder  
1 tsp baking powder  
2 tsp vanilla  
2 cups sugar  
2 cups flour  
1 pkg chocolate chips ( I like white)

Preheat oven to 325°. Whisk butter, eggs, cocoa, baking powder, vanilla until mixed well. Mix in flour and sugar. Add chocolate chips and mix well. Spread in greased 9 x 13 pan. Bake 30 minutes, or until an inserted toothpick comes out clean.
