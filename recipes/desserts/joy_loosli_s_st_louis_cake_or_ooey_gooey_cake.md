## Joy Loosli’s St. Louis Cake (or ooey-gooey cake) {#joy-loosli’s-st.-louis-cake-(or-ooey-gooey-cake)}

Ingredients:  
Crust layer:  
1 yellow or white cake mix  
1 egg  
1/2 c (1 stick) melted butter or margarine

Topping layer:  
8 oz. cream cheese (room temperature)  
1 tsp vanilla  
2 eggs  
1/2 c (1 stick) melted butter or margarine  
1 lb. (3 1/2 c) powdered sugar

Instructions:  
Preheat oven to 350 degrees.  
Mix cake mix, egg, melted butter; spread in 9 X 13" cake pan.  
Mix cream cheese, vanilla, eggs, butter, powdered sugar. Pour over crust layer; spread evenly  
Bake 45-50 minutes in preheated oven. Serve warm.
