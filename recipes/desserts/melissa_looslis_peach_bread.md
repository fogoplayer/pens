## Melissa Looslis’ Peach Bread {#melissa-looslis’-peach-bread}

1 ½ C Sugar  
2 Eggs  
½ C Shortening  
2 C mashed peaches  
 \- little juice  
2 C Flour  
1 tsp cinnamon  
1 tsp baking soda  
1 tsp baking powder  
1 tsp vanilla  
½ C nuts, if desired

Cream together sugar, eggs, shortening. Add rest of ingredients. Bake about 325 for 75 min 375\* for 35 minutes

For muffins, fill ⅔ full and bake 20 minutes
