## Rice Pudding {#rice-pudding}

2 c cooked rice  
2 eggs  
3/4 c milk  
1/4 c sugar  
1/2 tsp cinnamon  
1/2 c raisins/peaches/apples (optional)  
Preheat oven to 350\. Put a pan of water on the center oven rack. Mix eggs, milk, sugar, cinnamon in 1 qt. casserole dish. Stir in rice; cover dish. Place in pan of water; bake 45 minutes-1 hr., until knife tester comes out clean. Serve warm or cold.  
(Or you can microwave covered dish on high for 25 minutes, stirring after 10, 20 minutes.
