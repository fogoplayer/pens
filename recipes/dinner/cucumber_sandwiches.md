## Cucumber Sandwiches {#cucumber-sandwiches}

Ingredients  
8 oz. cream cheese, softened  
1/4 c. mayonnaise  
1 medium cucumber, peeied, seeded, \&amp: finely chopped  
1 T. chopped fresh dill  
1/4 tsp. garlic salt  
1 large English cucumber  
Approximately 12 to 16 slices good-quality sandwich bread (sourdough is the correct choice)

Directions  
1\. Place cream cheese and mayonnaise in a bowl; stir with a spoon or fork until  
creamy and free of lumps. Add dill and garlic salt; stir until well combined. Fold  
in finely chopped cucumber  
2\. Chill cream cheese mixture for about 2 hours for overnight) to allow flavors to  
meld.
