## Taco Soup {#taco-soup}

2 cans chicken broth  
1 can of drained chicken or ground beef  
1 package of taco seasoning  
1 package of ranch dressing mix  
1 can of undrained ranch style beans  
1 can corn  
1 can carrots  
1 can black/green beans

Put everything in a crock pot and simmer for a few hours. Simmer on low until ready to serve.
